## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Passes the slop test. Real project photography everywhere, a scroll-scrubbed hero film (all-intra encode, poster-first, mobile rendition, reduced-motion fallback), committed navy/charcoal industrial palette with amber held to accent-only, AA-guard ink tokens. Fraunces is on the reflex-reject list but is committed brand identity per PRODUCT.md — identity preservation wins. The amber-rule + tracked-caps kicker is a named brand system used deliberately; its density (hero, 4 page heroes, footer headings, project-detail sub-labels) is near the ceiling but consistent. The numbered 01–05 process is a genuine sequence, allowed. The one template moment: CredentialStrip's four identical icon+heading+text cells.

**Deterministic scan**: CLI scan of src/app + src/components: **0 findings (clean)**. In-page detector across 5 pages found:
- `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` ×10 (1 home, 5 contact, 4 about) — 1px border + ~40px shadow blur on white info/form cards (`border-concrete/*` + `shadow-card`). The one pattern both assessments independently flagged as the most template-shaped treatment.
- `image-hover-transform` ×16 (10 home, 6 projects) — the uniform hover zoom on every image card. Individually deliberate (premium Ken Burns move); flagged because it is applied identically to every image, which is a uniform reflex.
- `overused-font` — Fraunces at 34% of home text (19–27% elsewhere). Informational; brand-committed.
- `all-caps-body` ×1 (home) — the 38-char hero kicker. False positive as "body text" (it is a label), but corroborates the kicker-density note.

## Overall Impression
This is a genuinely premium marketing site that survives both slop tests: the photography is real, the hero film is distinctive, and the trust architecture (NCA registration number, KRA, FIDIC, OSH woven into content) is exactly what the audience needs. The single biggest opportunity is the mobile first fold — the primary audience is mobile-first, and the first paint gives them an incomplete headline, a small video sliver in a navy void, and nothing to tap.

## What's Working
1. **The hero film choreography** — scroll-scrubbed video with poster-first loading, a mobile rendition, and a reduced-motion fallback. Distinctive, on-brand, technically clean.
2. **Trust as featured content** — the NCA 1 reg number, KRA/OSH compliance, and FIDIC standards appear in the credential strip, footer badges, and stats, matching the "verify legitimacy" job of prospective clients.
3. **Craft depth below the surface** — contact-form draft persistence for interrupted mobile users, honeypot spam control, focus-trapped mobile menu, SSR-visible reveal content, print kill-switch, AA-safe `amber-ink`/`concrete-ink` tokens.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Mobile first fold has no CTA and an incomplete headline.** At 375px the first paint is: kicker, "We Build Landmark", a small clipped video, and a SCROLL cue — no tappable action (Request a Quote is desktop-only in the nav), no value proposition. The CTAs arrive ~1200px of scrub later. The primary audience is mobile-first on mid-tier connectivity. *Fix*: on mobile, either shorten the scrub dramatically, open the clip wider (e.g. 6–8% inset instead of 22%), or surface one CTA in the fold. *Suggested command*: /impeccable adapt.
2. **[P2] "Talk to our team" links to /services, not /contact.** The sixth card in the homepage services grid promises contact and delivers a brochure page. *Fix*: point it at /contact (one-line change). *Suggested command*: /impeccable clarify.
3. **[P2] The pinned hero cannot be skipped.** Every visit pays 1200px of scroll before content; the scroll cue fades but nothing accelerates repeat visitors. *Fix*: reduce SCROLL_HEIGHT, or let a tap/click on the cue jump past the choreography. *Suggested command*: /impeccable adapt or /impeccable animate.
4. **[P3] CredentialStrip is the weakest section visually** — four identical icon cells between otherwise photographic sections, and the detector's thin-border-wide-shadow hits cluster on the same white-card treatment (contact/about info cards). *Fix*: make credentials verifiable artifacts (registration numbers as document-style rows, certificate references) rather than icon cards; vary the card shadow recipe. *Suggested command*: /impeccable delight or /impeccable layout.
5. **[P3] Uniform hover-zoom on every image** (16 detector hits). One motion idea applied identically everywhere reads as reflex; consider letting project cards zoom but service cards do something else (caption slide, gradient shift). *Suggested command*: /impeccable animate.

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: First fold has zero tappable actions — the nav CTA is hidden on mobile and the hero CTAs are 1200px of scrub away. WhatsApp FAB is in the thumb zone (good). Draft persistence means an app-switch never loses the enquiry (excellent). The scrubbed video on a mid-tier phone is the riskiest performance surface on the site.

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: "Talk to our team" lands on /services — the one label/destination mismatch on the site. Everything else is plain language with labeled icons and an obvious next step after every section.

**Riley (Deliberate Stress Tester)**: The form holds up — blur validation with specific messages, server-error fallback naming an alternative channel, honeypot, work preserved on failure. Refresh mid-hero restores scroll position mid-choreography consistently. No broken empty states found.

## Minor Observations
- The contact email is a gmail.com address — a visible trust-signal gap for a "$10k-tier" brand (client decision, worth raising with them).
- Desktop fold shows only headline line 1 ("We Build Landmark") — an intentional tease that works compositionally there, but it makes the fold's message dependent on scrolling on every device.
- The projects "COMMERCIAL" badge on light-facade covers (Divine Suites) sits close to the legibility limit when the photo behind the badge zone is bright.
- Footer "Designed & built in Nairobi, Kenya." is a nice sign-off touch.

## Questions to Consider
- What would the mobile fold look like if it committed to the photograph full-bleed and let the film reveal be a desktop-only luxury?
- Could the credential strip show the actual paper — certificate crops, registry lookups — instead of icons saying "trust us"?
- If a padel-club owner lands on /services from WhatsApp, what is their one-tap path back to a human?
